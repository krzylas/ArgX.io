Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2025

Effective Date: November 25, 2025

Introduction

At Allergify, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Key Privacy Principles:

  • ⚡ Privacy First: Most data is stored locally on your device
  • 🔒 No Personal Data Collection: We don't collect names, emails, or personal identifiers
  • 🅿 Anonymous Usage: Product contributions are completely anonymous
  • ✅ Transparent: We clearly explain what data we process and why

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Stored Locally

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Allergen Preferences: Your selected allergens and dietary restrictions
  • Scan History: Products you've previously scanned
  • App Settings: Your preferences and configurations
  • Favorites: Products you've marked as favorites

1.2 Anonymous Product Data

When you contribute product information, we collect:

  • Product barcode numbers
  • Ingredient lists
  • Product photos (if you choose to upload)
  • Nutritional information

Important: This data is completely anonymous and cannot be linked to you personally.

1.3 Premium User Data (AI Analysis)

If you are a Premium subscriber using AI allergen analysis:

  • Ingredient text is sent to OpenAI for AI-powered analysis
  • This data is processed according to OpenAI's privacy policy
  • No personal identifiers are included in this data
  • Data is used solely for providing allergen analysis

1.4 Advertising Data (Free Users)

Free users see advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect:

  • Device information (device type, OS version)
  • Ad interaction data
  • General location (country/region level, not precise location)
  • Anonymous identifiers

This data is collected and processed by Google AdMob according to Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be crystal clear about what we don't collect:

  • ❌ Your name
  • ❌ Your email address
  • ❌ Your phone number
  • ❌ Your precise location
  • ❌ Your contacts
  • ❌ Your photos (except those you explicitly choose to upload for products)
  • ❌ Your health records or medical history
  • ❌ Any other personally identifiable information

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Local Data

Data stored on your device is used to:

  • Provide allergen alerts based on your preferences
  • Remember your scan history
  • Customize your app experience

3.2 Anonymous Product Contributions

Anonymous product data is used to:

  • Improve our product database
  • Help other users identify allergens
  • Share with Open Food Facts to benefit the broader community

3.3 AI Analysis (Premium)

Ingredient text sent to OpenAI is used to:

  • Provide advanced allergen detection
  • Offer health insights and recommendations
  • Improve analysis accuracy

4. Third-Party Services

Allergify integrates with the following third-party services:

4.1 Open Food Facts

  • Purpose: Product database
  • Data Shared: Anonymous product information
  • Privacy Policy: Open Food Facts Privacy

4.2 OpenAI (Premium Only)

  • Purpose: AI-powered allergen analysis
  • Data Shared: Ingredient text only
  • Privacy Policy: OpenAI Privacy Policy

4.3 Google AdMob (Free Users)

  • Purpose: Displaying advertisements
  • Data Shared: Device info, ad interactions
  • Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy

4.4 Apple App Store / Google Play Store

  • Purpose: Payment processing (Premium subscriptions)
  • Data Shared: Managed entirely by Apple/Google
  • Note: We never see your payment information

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Local Storage

Your personal preferences and scan history are stored securely on your device using industry-standard encryption.

5.2 Data Transmission

When data is transmitted (e.g., product contributions, AI analysis):

  • All communications use HTTPS encryption
  • Data is transmitted securely to prevent interception
  • No personal identifiers are included

5.3 Data Retention

  • Local Data: Stored until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Product Contributions: Retained indefinitely in our database and Open Food Facts
  • AI Analysis Data: Processed according to OpenAI's retention policy

6. Your Privacy Rights

6.1 Access and Control

  • View Your Data: All locally stored data can be viewed in the app settings
  • Delete Your Data: Uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Opt-Out of Ads: Upgrade to Premium to remove all advertisements

6.2 European Users (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights:

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

Note: Since we don't collect personal data, most GDPR rights are not applicable. You can always delete all local data by uninstalling the app.

6.3 California Users (CCPA)

California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Opt-out of the sale of personal information

Important: We do not collect or sell personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

Allergify requires users to be at least 13 years old (or 16 years old in the EEA).

  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under these ages
  • If we discover such collection, we will delete the data immediately
  • Parents/guardians should supervise children's use of the app

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be effective when:

  • We update the "Last Updated" date
  • We post the revised policy in the app
  • We notify you of material changes (in-app or via the app store)

Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new Privacy Policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: support@allergify.app
