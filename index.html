Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ArgX

📱 General

What does this app do?

ArgX scans food products by barcode and uses AI to identify over 100 allergens in ingredient lists, helping you make safer food choices.

Which devices are supported?

ArgX works on iPhone and iPad (iOS 16+). Android support is coming soon.

Is an account required?

No account is required to use basic scanning features.

🔍 Scanning & Allergens

How do I scan a product?

Tap the scan button on the home screen and point your camera at the product barcode.

What allergens can the app detect?

ArgX identifies over 100 common allergens including peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, gluten, soy, and more. You can customize your profile in Settings.

Can I add custom allergens?

Currently, you can select from our in-app list. Custom allergen entries are planned for future updates.

What if a barcode isn't found?

If a product isn't in our database, you'll see an option to submit details and help improve our data.

📊 Ingredient & Data Accuracy

Where does product information come from?

We primarily use Open Food Facts, an open, community-driven database. AI is used to interpret and cross-check ingredient lists for the most reliable results.

How accurate are allergen warnings?

ArgX aims for high accuracy, but results depend on product label quality and database completeness. Always double-check real packaging and consult your allergist for safety.

Does the app work with international products?

Yes—ingredients in many languages are supported. If you encounter an unsupported product, you can submit it for review.

🔐 Privacy & Data

What data does the app store?

Only anonymous scan data (barcode and ingredient information) is saved to improve the database. No personal information is collected.

Is my data shared?

No personal data is ever shared. Contributed product info may be sent to Open Food Facts to help the broader community.

Where can I read your privacy policy?

See our Privacy Policy in the app menu or on our website.

💎 Account & Premium Features

Is the app free?

Basic scanning is free. ArgX offers a premium subscription for advanced features like detailed nutritional analysis, personalized allergen profiles, and offline mode.
